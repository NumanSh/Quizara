## Design Context

### Users
Quizara serves Arabic- and English-speaking trivia players, primarily on mobile, who want to jump into a short solo quiz, compete live, challenge friends, and return for rankings and rewards. The interface must make the next useful action obvious without making experienced players wade through decoration.

### Brand Personality
Intelligent, kinetic, and confident. Quizara should feel like a live knowledge arena: energetic enough for competition, polished enough to feel credible, and warm enough for casual groups. It must not feel childish, toy-like, noisy, or like a generic neon gaming dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
A cinematic editorial game interface with generous negative space, strong typography, warm electric-lime accents, deep ink neutrals, and a restrained “knowledge orbit” motif. Motion references the pacing and spatial confidence of modern motion-led showcase sites while remaining appropriate for a functional trivia product. Support both LTR and RTL. Dark mode is the primary experience.

### Design Principles
1. Let content breathe: fewer boxed surfaces, larger section intervals, and clear editorial hierarchy.
2. Make motion explain the experience: orchestrated entrances, directional hover feedback, and state transitions—not ambient movement everywhere.
3. Keep competition legible: game modes, progress, scores, and calls to action must read instantly on small screens.
4. Use one memorable visual language: orbit lines, indexed labels, sharp lime accents, and deep warm neutrals instead of unrelated gradients and glows.
5. Preserve performance and inclusion: animate transforms and opacity, adapt layouts rather than hiding functionality, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
